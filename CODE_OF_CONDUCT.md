# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We are committed to providing a welcoming and harassment-free experience for
everyone, regardless of background or identity.

## Our standards

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Accept constructive criticism gracefully.
- Focus on what is best for the community.

## Enforcement

Instances of unacceptable behavior may be reported to the maintainers. All
complaints will be reviewed and investigated.

This Code of Conduct is adapted from the Contributor Covenant.
